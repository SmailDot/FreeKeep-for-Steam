Automatically claim free-to-keep Steam games and DLC giveaways in the background, using the Steam login your browser already has. No password, no cookies, no server.

🔒 WHY IT'S SAFE, IN PLAIN WORDS
• Your browser already knows you're logged in to Steam. It attaches that login by itself to requests to store.steampowered.com, just like when you browse the store. FreeKeep only asks it to send the same request as Steam's own "Add to Account" button.
• FreeKeep can't see your login. Steam keeps it in a locked (HttpOnly) cookie that no extension can read, and FreeKeep doesn't even have the cookies permission.
• There is no FreeKeep server. Nothing is uploaded, so nothing can leak.
• Check it yourself: chrome://extensions → FreeKeep → Details lists only notifications and store.steampowered.com. The code is open source.

MANY AUTO-CLAIMERS vs FREEKEEP
❌ Ask for your password, token or cookies → ✅ Never
❌ Open a store tab for every game and click for you → ✅ One quiet background request, no tabs
❌ Run on a server or a bot → ✅ Runs only in your own browser
❌ Send data to their server → ✅ No server, no analytics, no tracking

WHAT IT DOES
• Claims only real limited-time free-to-keep promotions, never demos, playtests or free-to-play games
• Skips games you already own, and claims DLC only when you own the base game
• Checks every 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours, plus once when the browser starts
• Claims automatically, or notifies you first so you can decide
• One quiet notification when something is claimed or needs you
• Optional Epic Games Store reminders: get told when Epic gives a game away, then claim it yourself on Epic's site. Off by default; it reads Epic's public list without any login
• 12 languages, about 40 KB of code, zero dependencies

Log in to the Steam store in this browser and FreeKeep does the rest.
