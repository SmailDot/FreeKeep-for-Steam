Automatically claims limited-time free Steam games and DLC in the background, using the Steam session already in your browser. No password, no access to your login cookie, no server.

🔒 WHY IT'S SAFE
• Reuses your browser session: once you're signed in to Steam, your browser attaches your session to requests to the Steam store. FreeKeep only sends the same request as Steam's "Add to Account" button.
• Can't read your credentials: Steam protects its login cookie with the HttpOnly flag, so no extension can read it. FreeKeep doesn't request the cookies permission either.
• No server: FreeKeep has no backend and doesn't upload or collect any data.
• Verifiable: the extension's Details page in Chrome shows only notifications and access to the Steam store. The source code is public.

MANY AUTO-CLAIMERS vs FREEKEEP
❌ Require your password or login cookies → ✅ Not needed
❌ Open a store tab for each game → ✅ A single background request, no tabs
❌ Run on a server or bot → ✅ Runs only in your browser
❌ Send data to the developer's server → ✅ No server, no analytics, no tracking

FEATURES
• Claims only genuine limited-time free-to-keep promotions; demos, playtests and free-to-play games are excluded
• Skips games you already own and claims DLC only if you own the base game
• Uses your Steam store region, so it claims only what's free in your region
• Checks every 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours, and at browser startup
• Claims automatically, or asks you first
• Notifies you only when a game is claimed or needs your attention
• Optional Epic Games Store reminders: tells you about Epic's free games so you can claim them on Epic's site. Off by default; the public list is read without signing in
• 12 languages, about 40 KB of code, no dependencies

Sign in to the Steam store in this browser and FreeKeep runs automatically.
