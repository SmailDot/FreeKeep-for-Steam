# Security policy

FreeKeep runs with the user's Steam store session, so security reports are taken seriously.

Please report vulnerabilities **privately** by email to [smaildot@aidot.me](mailto:smaildot@aidot.me)
or through GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new), not in public
issues. You should get a reply within a few days.

In scope: anything that could let FreeKeep, another extension or a website read, store or send a
user's Steam credentials or session, make FreeKeep contact a host other than
`store.steampowered.com`, or perform account actions other than claiming free packages.
